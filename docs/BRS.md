# Business Requirements Specification – ControlTune

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-1 | Identify a dynamic model of a plant from recorded input–output data and report how well it predicts held-out data. |
| BR-2 | Tune a PI controller on the model, and compare a nominal tuning with one that accounts for model uncertainty. |
| BR-3 | Report stability and performance of both tunings on uncertainty sets that were not used for tuning (including plant drift). |
| BR-4 | State clearly that the evaluation is on identified models, not on the plant. |

Out of scope: closed-loop experiments on the plant, multivariable control, constraints, anti-windup.
